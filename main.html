District 404: Code To Survive

The arena opens once more. In District 404, survival is no longer about strength or speed it’s about innovation, strategy, and the courage to build when everything around you is falling apart

ABOUT DISTRICT 404 V1.0

Welcome to District 404 a survival-themed hackathon Where innovation becomes your only weapon. Inspired by the world of the Hunger Games, this event transforms participants into digital tributes, facing thematic challenges designed to test creativity, speed, and resilience. Compete as individuals or teams (your districts) and use your coding, design, and problem-solving abilities to overcome missions centred around real-world issues.

TRACKS AND THEMES

what to learn?

TRACK 1
DESIGN

This track is dedicated entirely to visual design and user experience. No coding required. Participants focus on creating intuitive, elegant, and impactful interfaces that solve the sponsor’s problem. You’ll build prototypes, design systems, and user flows that transform complexity into clarity.

TRACK 2
FRONT-END

This track challenges participants to transform design into functional, responsive interfaces using modern front-end technologies. Your mission is to code the user-facing part of the solution: fast, clean, and accessible.

TRACK 3
BACK-END

This track focuses on building the server-side logic, APIs, data handling, and system architecture. Participants create the backbone that makes the application functional, secure, and reliable.
